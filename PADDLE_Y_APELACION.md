# Paddle: categoría, descripción y apelación

## 1. Categoría del producto en Paddle

Al crear/editar el producto en el panel Live (o en la solicitud):

- **Category:** Consumer Software / **Gaming** (o la más cercana a videojuegos / software de entretenimiento)
- **No** uses categorías de marketing, advertising, social media growth, etc.

### Product name
```
TokBid Ranking Game
```

### Product description (inglés — para el panel de Paddle)
```
TokBid is an independent entertainment ranking game. Players spend small amounts to
move entries up a fun community leaderboard on tokbid.lol.

This is not a marketing, advertising, or social-media growth service.
TokBid is not affiliated with TikTok or any social network.
Payments only affect the in-game leaderboard on our website.
They do not increase real followers, views, or reach on any external platform.
```

### Tax category (si pregunta)
Standard digital goods / software — según lo que ofrezca el panel.

## 2. Borrador de apelación (inglés)

Subject: Appeal – tokbid.lol product category (entertainment ranking game)

```
Hello Paddle team,

I am writing to appeal the decision regarding tokbid.lol.

TokBid is an independent entertainment product: a social ranking game where users
can participate for fun on our website leaderboard. It is not an advertising agency,
marketing plan, or social-media growth service.

Important clarifications:
• TokBid is not affiliated with TikTok or any social network.
• There is no technical integration that boosts real accounts.
• Purchases only change position on our own in-game leaderboard at tokbid.lol.
• We do not promise or deliver increased followers, views, or reach on external platforms.
• The website now states this clearly for all users.

We believe the correct product category is Consumer Software / Gaming
(entertainment), not advertising or marketing services.

We have updated the public website copy to reflect this positioning.
We kindly ask you to reconsider our application under the entertainment / gaming category.

Thank you for your time.
Best regards,
Raimundo
tokbid.lol
```

## 4. Antes de enviar la apelación

1. Despliega los nuevos textos en producción.
2. Abre tokbid.lol y comprueba que se ve el aviso del Hero y el del buscador.
3. Envía la apelación **después** de que la web ya muestre el nuevo enfoque.
